Questions

Do I need to provide water or power?

Our mobile setup carries its own water and power, so you don't need to provide either.

Where can you clean my vehicle?

At your home or workplace, as long as there's a suitable space to clean your vehicle.

Which package should I choose?

Gold is our recommended first visit. If you're not sure, send us a photo and we'll recommend what's needed.

Can I book the £20 Quick Exterior Wash straight away?

It's available after your first valet, and it's perfect between your 4 to 6 week maintenance valets.

Is decontamination included in Bronze?

Full decontamination isn't included in Bronze. Full exterior decontamination and iron fallout removal are part of Gold.

Do you do paint correction?

Paint correction and restoration aren't included in our packages. Send us a photo and we'll advise you.

How do I book?

Message us on Facebook, call, text or WhatsApp 07546 819467. Our latest availability is posted on our Facebook page. Tell us your preferred time when booking.
